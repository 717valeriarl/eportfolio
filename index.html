Hello!

I'm a first-year Computer Engineering student at Georgia Tech. In high school I was part of VEX robotics for 675A, I spent four weeks at the Georgia Governor's Honors Program building Butler Bot, a robot meant to drive itself around and bring you a cold drink! I built its voice-detecting audio array on a Raspberry Pi and programmed its interface on Google Coral.

My current thread choices are Distributed Systems & Software Design and Telecommunications. Computing Hardware & Emerging Architecture is my backup, since I'd like to understand the computers those systems run on. In Summer 2027 I'll intern at Amazon through the Amazon Future Engineer scholarship program.

I grew up speaking English and Spanish, and I'm currently learning Korean at Tech. I was also Vice President of Polaris Peer Leaders and Exec Parliamentarian of Student Council in high school. In my free time, I like to crochet, paint banners, and host events.

Butler Bot

Summer 2025 · Georgia Governor's Honors Program, ECE · Team project · Raspberry Pi, Google Coral

Butler Bot was a four-week team project where we designed, coded, and built a robot from scratch. The goal was a robot that could drive itself and bring the user a refrigerated drink. My part was the voice and the interface. I designed and built an audio array on a Raspberry Pi so the robot could detect the user's voice, and I programmed and built the GUI on a Google Coral so people could interact with it.
